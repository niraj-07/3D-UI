# TaskBubble — IEEE follow-up notes

Proposed work after the [hackathon version](PRD_HACKATHON_MVP.md). These ideas are not implemented features or validated research findings.

## Research question

Does delegating a constrained edit from VR to a desktop collaborator improve coordination, accuracy, or perceived control compared with VR-only or direct shared editing?

The current prototype provides component selection, delegation, precise single-axis rotation, live contextual previews, revision, and VR approval. It is a starting point for that comparison.

## Possible extensions

- More meaningful assembly tasks with clearance or alignment constraints.
- Additional allowed operations, such as constrained translation.
- A small catalog of components that desktop can propose adding at defined attachment points, with VR preview and approval.
- Complete assembly-state recovery after disconnection.
- Clearer ownership and revision cues.

Choose extensions based on the research question. More editing features alone do not establish a new interaction technique.

## Evaluation

Review related desktop–VR collaboration and task-handoff systems. Compare selected tasks against simpler alternatives, measuring completion time, errors, coordination effort, and perceived control. Report user observations and performance measurements separately from expectations.

Check the [official contest page](https://ieeevr.org/2027/contribute/3dui-contest/) for submission requirements and dates.
